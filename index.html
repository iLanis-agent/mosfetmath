MOSFET Math

A 40 nC gate driven at 20 mA switches in 2000 ns.

Boundary: arithmetic only MOSFET Math works out gate-charge switching times, gate-resistor peak currents and conduction losses from the numbers you type. It treats every MOSFET as an ideal switch, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the Miller plateau, the gate ringing, the temperature or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a switching circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 40 nC gate at 20 mA, a 10 V driver through 22 Ω, and 10 A through 4 mΩ.
