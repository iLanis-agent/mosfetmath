MOSFET Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out gate-charge switching times, gate-resistor peak currents and conduction losses from the numbers you type, using the chart relationships charge is current times time, voltage is current times resistance, and power is current squared times resistance. The charges, currents, voltages and resistances are yours to measure or read off your own datasheet. It treats every MOSFET as an ideal switch, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the Miller plateau, the gate ringing, the temperature or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a switching circuit is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The switching truth

How fast the gate charges: total gate charge over the current the driver delivers.

The resistor truth

The peak current into the gate: driver voltage over the total gate resistance.

The conduction truth

What the on-state costs: drain current squared times the on-resistance.
